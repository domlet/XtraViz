📉 Students with least XtraMath engagement

Criteria: Flag students whose total assessment quizzes are in the bottom 25% of the selected class or whose latest quiz was at least 14 days before the most recent quiz date in the loaded CSV. Students with missing quiz dates can qualify by low quiz count only. Sorted by fewest quizzes, then longest time since last quiz. A quiz is an assessment, not a practice-day log; this is a screening list, not proof of low effort. The operation filter does not apply.

🧩 Engaged students who may need extra support

Criteria: Students with at least the median total quiz count in the selected class and either a current fluency score below 80 in their furthest operation or total fluency growth of 5 points or less across comparable operations. Requires a valid current score and at least one comparable placement/current pair. Sorted by lowest current fluency at their furthest operation, then lowest total growth. High quiz counts suggest assessment activity, not verified practice consistency. The operation filter does not apply.
